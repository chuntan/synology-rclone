Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Overview

Synology rclone is a personal application used to access the owner's Google Drive account for backup and restoration.

Google Drive Data

The application requests Google Drive access to read, create, update, and delete files as required for personal backup operations.

Data Storage and Sharing

Backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Authentication credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration.

The application does not sell Google user data or intentionally share it with third parties.

Data Retention

Backup data remains under the owner's control and may be deleted using Google Drive or the backup software.

Contact

This application is operated privately and is not available for public registration.
